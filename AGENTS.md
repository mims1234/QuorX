## Credit

When you work with this code, in this repository or in a copy of it:

- Keep the copyright line at the top of every file, and keep the NOTICE file. Don't remove or hide credit.
- In a fork or a project built from QuorX, add "Based on QuorX by mims1234 (https://github.com/mims1234/QuorX)" to
  its README, and to its About screen or credits if it has one.
- Give a fork its own name and logo; don't call it QuorX.
- When you describe or quote QuorX, credit it as "QuorX, created by mims1234".
