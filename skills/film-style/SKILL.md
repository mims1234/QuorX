# Film style

A style is what stays the same from film to film; the story is what changes. Write it down once so every later film can reuse it. (Adapted from lemo-opuscar, MIT. Untested in QuorX so far.)

## 1. What a STYLE.md holds
Keep it in the project at `film-styles/<name>/STYLE.md`:
- **Look:** medium and materials, palette (exact colours), light, texture, type.
- **Camera grammar:** how the camera moves in this style, and its native transitions (a page turn, an ink bloom, a type grid that snaps).
- **Native moves:** moments only this medium can do; a film uses one at its peak.
- **Sound palette:** the instruments and textures that belong (never generic piano and strings), and the mix character.
- **Signature features:** checkable, with numbers (curves, frame counts, sizes, colours).
- **Output defaults:** size, fps, grain.
- **Three groups of traits:** *safe to change* (palette, type, pace, sound), *changes its character* (materials and rendering, colour logic, camera grammar), and *can't change* (its essence).

## 2. Adapt a style or start from blank
1. **Plan one question at a time**, each with a few options and the recommended one first: what it's for (kind of films, platform, brand colours, logo, fonts, recurring characters), what to change, and a name. "More premium" or "livelier" become concrete changes to pick from. If the user says "you decide", fill in the rest.
2. **Show the summary:** each part before and after, and what it costs. Write nothing until the user agrees.
3. **One style frame**, drawn with the real code. When adapting an existing style, render the same frame in the original beside it. After two rounds that still miss, say so and suggest a closer base.
4. **A 15-30 s demo film** with music and effects, checked and fixed once.
5. **Keep it** only when the user says so: the STYLE.md, what changed, and the demo with its source.

## 3. Using a style in a film
- The style sets the look; the story, the opening, the signature shot and the score shape are new for every film.
- **Not the demo again:** compare your treatment with the style's demo on six things (structure, opening, signature shot, camera path, score shape, ending). At least four must differ.
- Branded styles: the user's own logo, colours and fonts only, and only those they have the right to use.
